About the author

J.W.Skinner

I write stories about the people we become when life asks too much of us.

The bio

John is a writer who lives in Texas with his wife Carrie, their four children who are grown and living their own lives, and two dogs, Gizmo and Copper.

He writes stories about people who are trying to find their way back to themselves, after loss, after silence, after the kind of grief that doesn’t announce itself but simply becomes the weather of your days.

The Anchor and the Tide is his debut novel. It was written in the quiet hours, for anyone who has ever had to learn the difference between surviving something and actually living again.

His work has grown beyond a single book and a single genre. Alongside contemporary fiction, he is building The Morgan Legacy, a five-book Western saga about family, land, inheritance, violence, and the consequences one generation leaves for the next.

Life outside fiction

Beyond the books.

Professional recognition · August 2024

Marquis Who’s Who

Honored Listee

John was recognized as an Honored Listee by Marquis Who’s Who in August 2024.

Before publishing fiction, John spent years solving a different kind of problem. His career spans 25 years across information technology and cybersecurity, from technical support and systems administration to security operations and protecting organizations against digital threats. He currently works as an Information Security Analyst with Balfour Beatty US.

Teaching has been another important part of that work. As a Cybersecurity Lead Instructor with edX, John taught in university-affiliated programs at the University of Kansas and Northwestern University, helping students develop practical skills and prepare for careers in cybersecurity. He has also developed educational content for EC-Council, including Implementing Cybersecurity with Bash Scripting.

John earned a master’s degree in Computer Science with a focus in Cybersecurity Engineering from Colorado Technical University, graduating with a 4.0 GPA. He also holds a bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, earned summa cum laude, and professional certifications spanning cybersecurity, security analysis, cloud security, and artificial intelligence.

Outside his professional life, John spent more than 25 years serving Mesquite Baseball in volunteer leadership roles, including Executive Vice President. Alongside writing, family, and life in Texas, teaching and community service are important parts of the person behind the books.

Why these stories

The setting changes. The question underneath doesn’t.

What happens after the thing you thought would break you? Who are you when the old version of your life stops working?

That question can live on a cruise ship. It can live on a Texas ranch. It can live in a romance, a Western, or whatever world comes next. The genre is the landscape. The people are the point.
