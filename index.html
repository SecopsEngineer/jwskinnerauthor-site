The author behind the worlds

Meet J.W. Skinner.

John writes about people trying to find their way back to themselves, after loss, after silence, after the kind of grief that doesn’t announce itself but simply becomes the weather of your days.

He lives in Texas with his wife Carrie. Their four children are grown and building lives of their own, while Gizmo and Copper still run the home office with questionable management practices.
