Book One

A man can bury the gunfighter. The past may still know where to find him.

Levi Morgan built a ranch, a family, and a life intended to put distance between himself and the man he once had to be. But the Texas plains remember violence, and a threat on the horizon has a way of making old skills useful again.

Blood on the Texas Plains opens The Morgan Legacy, a five-book saga of land, family, inheritance, sacrifice, and the choices that outlive the people who make them.
