The story

Sometimes the hardest thing to survive is the life that comes after.

The Anchor and the Tide follows two people carrying private grief onto an eleven-day voyage. What begins as distance becomes recognition. What begins as survival becomes the possibility of a life neither expected to want again.

The novel is rooted in the quiet spaces between loss and recovery, and in the uncomfortable truth that healing rarely arrives looking the way we planned.
